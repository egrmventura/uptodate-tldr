Iterate on the most recent change until it meets its goal. Goal: take the current working diff (or a named module) and run a tight refine loop — run the relevant tests, read the failure or the output, make the smallest correct fix, re-run, and repeat until green or until the change demonstrably does what it was meant to do. Key constraint: change only what the current goal requires (no scope creep, no unrelated refactors), keep each iteration small enough to attribute cause and effect, and stop as soon as the exit condition holds rather than gold-plating. Validate by ending with the target tests passing (or the app producing the expected observable output) and a one-line summary of what changed across the iterations and why it now satisfies the goal.
